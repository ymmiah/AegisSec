# Severity and Prioritisation

Do not reduce a finding to a scanner score. Consider:
- exploit prerequisites;
- exposure and reachable attack surface;
- privileges obtained;
- data sensitivity;
- business process affected;
- control compensations;
- detectability;
- threat intelligence and active exploitation evidence;
- recovery impact.

Use CVSS when appropriate, but state environmental/business modifiers separately. Prioritise remediation by expected risk reduction, not label alone.
