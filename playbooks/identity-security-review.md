# Identity Security Review

1. Define identity systems, tenants/domains and ownership.
2. Inventory privileged, human, service and workload identities.
3. Review authentication, federation, Conditional Access, legacy protocols and recovery accounts.
4. Review privilege, PAM/PIM, delegated access and app/service-principal consent.
5. Review lifecycle, access reviews, dormant identities and machine credentials.
6. Review logging/detection and identity incident response.
7. Prioritise attack-path choke points.
8. Plan change-controlled remediation and retest.
