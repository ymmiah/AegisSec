# Exposure Management

1. Establish asset and ownership truth.
2. Correlate vulnerability, identity, configuration, internet exposure and cloud context.
3. Validate material findings and remove duplicates.
4. Build exposure/attack paths to critical assets.
5. Rank by exploitability, reachability, privilege, business impact and threat activity.
6. Remediate choke points that break the most paths.
7. Verify and measure risk reduction over time.
