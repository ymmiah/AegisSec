# Incident Response

1. Declare owner/severity and protect communications.
2. Preserve critical logs, snapshots and volatile evidence proportionately.
3. Scope impacted identities, systems, data and business processes.
4. Contain the most dangerous active paths using reversible actions where possible.
5. Remove root cause and exposed credentials/secrets.
6. Restore from trusted state with validation gates.
7. Increase monitoring during recovery.
8. Conduct lessons learned; update controls/detections/runbooks.
