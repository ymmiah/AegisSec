# Ransomware Response

1. Prioritise human safety and critical-service continuity.
2. Isolate affected segments/endpoints while preserving evidence.
3. Protect backups, hypervisors, identity systems and management planes.
4. Identify initial access and active attacker paths.
5. Rotate compromised privileged credentials in a controlled order.
6. Rebuild/restore from known-good state; do not trust merely decrypted systems.
7. Validate business data, identity and monitoring before reconnecting.
8. Conduct lessons learned and close root causes.
